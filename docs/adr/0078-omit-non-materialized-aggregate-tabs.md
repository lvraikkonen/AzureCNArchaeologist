# Omit Non-materialized Aggregate Tabs

Status: Accepted

A Category option labeled `All` or `全部` whose declared target panel is absent is a general source interaction aggregate rather than a concrete content state. v0.4 omits it from the Business Payload option catalog and Reachability Relation, records the suppression in source evidence, and promotes the first remaining concrete sibling to that scope's default; it never synthesizes an aggregate group, copies sibling prices, or emits a placeholder. Missing targets that do not satisfy this exact aggregate identity remain blocking because the aggregate convention cannot authorize unrelated source loss.
