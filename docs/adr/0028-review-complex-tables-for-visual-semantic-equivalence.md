# Review Complex Tables for Visual Semantic Equivalence

Status: Accepted

Complex Table Visual Review judges Visual Semantic Equivalence rather than pixel equality. The rendered Business Payload must preserve hierarchical headers, merged-cell boundaries, reading order, price-to-label alignment, units and qualifiers, footnote attachment, full visibility, and legibility; CMS-specific typography, color, spacing, and border changes are acceptable when they do not change that meaning. Screenshot and pixel-difference artifacts may help the reviewer but do not decide the outcome, because requiring identical source and CMS styling would reject legitimate presentation changes while failing to focus review on pricing interpretation.
