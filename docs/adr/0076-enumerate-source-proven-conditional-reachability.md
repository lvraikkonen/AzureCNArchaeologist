# Enumerate Source-Proven Conditional Reachability

Status: Accepted

Filtered v0.4 reconstruction uses the deterministic Reachability Relation independently proven by each frozen Source Snapshot rather than the Cartesian product of flattened option catalogs. Independent domains may be multiplied only within their common scope; a Conditional Filter Domain such as software-specific Category tabs contributes only its selected parent branch's options, order, and default. Every proven reachable tuple maps to exactly one complete active `contentGroup`, and CMS import treats that exact group relation as its rendering authority rather than generating additional option combinations. Theoretical cross-branch combinations, unreachable DOM panels, placeholders, and Payload-inferred reconstruction completeness are rejected. This supersedes ADR-0045 because real supported pages expose dependent selection topologies that a global product would misrepresent.
