# Only Reachable Pricing Facts Are Reconstructed

Status: Accepted

Pricing Fact Fidelity covers the price-bearing content exposed through Reachable Selection States, not every hidden pricing fragment present in the raw DOM. Price-bearing content is Orphan Pricing Evidence only when evidence affirmatively proves it belongs to no reachable state; mere inability to assign applicability remains a blocking Pricing Fidelity Evaluation Failure. A proven orphan is retained and reported but not imported or treated as missing, while Snapshot-bound Interaction Evidence that proves a state was reachable reclassifies the content as required. Importing any orphan is a reconstruction failure. An orphan explicitly evidenced as intentionally disabled, archived, or deprecated is Explained; one whose unreachability is proven but whose reason is unknown is Unresolved and blocks approval pending Source Finding Disposition.
