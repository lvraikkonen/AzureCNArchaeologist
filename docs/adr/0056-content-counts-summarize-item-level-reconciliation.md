# Content Counts Summarize Item-level Reconciliation

Status: Accepted

Table, FAQ, and similar content metrics are Content Reconciliation Summaries derived from item-level matching rather than page-wide equality gates. Reports separate source physical, expected logical, payload physical, projected logical, matched, missing, extra, duplicate, changed, misassigned, global, and orphan counts; FAQ units are complete Question/Answer pairs rather than incidental DOM nodes. Raw totals may differ through global projection or justified orphan exclusion, but every delta must be explained, and any unexplained item-level loss, invention, duplication, change, or misassignment is blocking.
