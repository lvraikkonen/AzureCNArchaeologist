# Classify Orphans by Explanation and Approval Impact

Status: Accepted

Content is Orphan Pricing Evidence only after evidence affirmatively proves it belongs to no Reachable Selection State; mere applicability ambiguity remains a blocking Pricing Fidelity Evaluation Failure. Every proven orphan stays outside the Expected Pricing Fact Inventory and Business Payload, and importing it is a blocking error. An orphan with explicit frozen-source evidence that it was intentionally disabled, archived, or deprecated is Explained: it produces a warning and upstream report but does not fail Machine Validation or block approval. An orphan whose unreachability is proven but whose reason or ownership remains unknown is Unresolved: faithful reconstruction may pass, but `approval_eligible=false` until Source Finding Disposition. Treating every hidden fragment as publishable, every ambiguity as an orphan, or every orphan as equally blocking was rejected.
